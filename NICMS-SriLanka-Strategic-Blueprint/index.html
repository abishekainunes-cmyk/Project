Core platform features

Zero-knowledge privacy

Your data is encrypted end-to-end. Even system administrators cannot read your case details without your explicit authorization.

Justice Key — anonymous tracking

Receive a 16-digit key to track your case from any device. No login, no email, no digital footprint that an abuser can find.

Evidence time-capsule

Every uploaded file receives a cryptographic checksum (SHA-256) that proves it was never altered — a silent, untampered court witness.

Offline-first resilience

In areas with poor connectivity, your report is saved locally and synced automatically when the signal returns. Nothing is lost.

Digital handshake protocol

Every inter-agency referral requires formal acceptance within 4 hours. Accountability is not optional — it's built into the code.

Panic button — instant exit

A persistent "Quick Exit" button replaces the site instantly with a weather search. Visible to you, invisible to anyone behind you.
