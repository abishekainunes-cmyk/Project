How the Hybrid Scoring Works
When a user browses, cold-start items with few interactions leverage Random Forest regressors trained on tabular metadata (price point, sentiment polarity, brand tier). For established users, SVD decomposes historical rating matrices into latent semantic vectors, blending both to eliminate sparsity blindspots.
