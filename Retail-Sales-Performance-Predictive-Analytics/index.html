The Problem & Approach

In retail, stockouts destroy immediate revenue while overstocking ties up critical working capital. This case study addresses this inventory distortion:

  • Business Analyst Lifecycle: Authored comprehensive BRDs and FRDs aligning turnover ratio goals with engineering execution.
  • SQL Heavy Lifting: Built multi-stage data pipelines using Window Functions and CTEs to produce rolling 7-day and 30-day demand averages.
  • Agile Delivery: Executed in sprints, validating model behavior against retail promotions and seasonality.

Model Forecasting & Tech Stack

Leveraged Python statistical modeling alongside Power BI for executive self-service decision making:

  • ARIMA & Prophet Models: Evaluated time-series models against real historical demand variance.
  • ABC/XYZ Classification: Identified top 20% SKU drivers producing 70% of profit margins.
  • Automated Dashboards: Converted manual spreadsheets into real-time self-updating analytics.
